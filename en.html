[ Confirmation & Pledges for Program Participation ]

  • Costs: I will pay the monthly studio rent, studio fire & liability insurance (JPY 1,000/month, tax incl.), and utilities (short-term: per the Center's standard flat-rate schedule / long-term: per actual meter readings) by their due dates.
  • Key deposit & restoration: A key deposit is required upon move-in. Upon move-out, I will restore the studio to its original condition and return it.
  • Short-term duration: The short-term program (3 months) cannot be extended; all short-term residents move out and restore their studios by May 31, 2027.
  • Rules & community: I will comply with Koganecho Area Management Center's management rules and studio regulations, and maintain respectful relations with neighbors and fellow resident artists.
  • International travel: Airfare, living expenses, overseas travel insurance, and visa acquisition are my own responsibility. I understand Japanese immigration regulations and that selling artworks on non-working visas is in principle prohibited.
  • Personal information: Personal information provided here will be used solely for the selection and administration of this Open Call and the AIR program.
